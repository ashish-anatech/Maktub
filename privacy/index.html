Privacy Policy

How Maktub Mobile Notary handles the information you send through this site.

Information we collect

When you submit the appointment request form, we collect the name, phone number, email address, service type, preferred date and time, location, and any message you choose to provide.

How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your request, confirm your appointment, and quote your total cost. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Notary journal records

Washington notaries are required to keep a journal of notarial acts. Journal entries are maintained and retained as state law requires, separately from this website.

Sharing

  • We do not sell or rent personal information.
  • Information may be shared where required by law or by a court order.
  • Service providers who help operate this site may process data on our behalf.

Your choices

You may ask us to delete the details you submitted through the form at any time by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
