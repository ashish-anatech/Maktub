Questions

Before you book

Washington caps the notarial act itself at $15 for a traditional or electronic in-person notarization, and $25 for a remote online notarization. If we travel to you, a mobile travel fee is added based on distance, and evening, weekend, or rush appointments add a scheduling fee. Every figure is published on the pricing page and confirmed before your appointment.

Either works. If you come to us, you pay only the notarial act fee. If we travel to your home, office, hospital, or care facility, a mobile travel fee is added: $35 within about 10 miles, $50 for 10 to 20 miles, $70 for 20 to 30 miles, and $60 to $90 for hospital and nursing home visits.

Bring your current, government-issued photo ID and the complete document, unsigned. Washington notaries must witness the signature, so please do not sign ahead of time. If the document names additional signers or witnesses, they need to be present with their own photo ID.

You join a scheduled video session on a secure platform. Your identity is verified electronically, you sign the document on screen, and the notarial act is recorded as Washington requires for remote online notarization. The fee is $25 per notarial act.
