What It Covers

The notarizations people need most

Most general notary work is straightforward, which is exactly why it deserves attention. A missing journal entry or an incorrectly worded certificate is the sort of thing nobody notices until the document is rejected.

Bring the document unsigned and a current government-issued photo ID, and the appointment usually takes a few minutes.

  • Acknowledgments and jurats
  • Affidavits and sworn statements
  • Powers of attorney
  • Copy certifications and signature witnessing
  • Vehicle titles, consent forms, and school or employment paperwork

Before You Arrive

Four things to have ready

Current photo ID

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID for every person signing.

The complete document

All pages, including the notarial certificate. Nothing left blank.

An unsigned signature line

Washington notaries must witness the signing, so please wait until we are together.

Any additional signers
