Who this is for

Some signings do not need anyone to be in the same room. Washington permits remote online notarization, where identity is verified electronically and the signing takes place in a recorded video session.

This is the right service if you are out of the area, short on time, working across time zones, or simply would rather not arrange a visit, and if the party receiving your document accepts a remote online notarization. That last point is worth confirming before you book.

When It Helps

No travel, no travel fee

Remote online notarization removes the logistics from the appointment. There is no drive, no meeting point, and no mobile travel fee, just a scheduled session you join from a phone, tablet, or computer.

It suits out-of-state signers, tight timelines, mobility limits, and anyone who would simply rather not coordinate an in-person visit.

  • Signers located anywhere, for documents used in Washington
  • Electronic identity verification built into the session
  • The session is recorded, as Washington requires
  • No mobile travel fee, $25 per notarial act
  • Rush sessions available for an additional $25

What You Need

Four things before the session

A device with a camera

A phone, tablet, or computer with a working camera, microphone, and stable internet.

Current photo ID

An unexpired government-issued photo ID for the electronic verification step.

The document, unsigned

Send it ahead so it can be prepared for signing during the session.

A quiet few minutes

Somewhere you can see and hear clearly for the length of the session.

Not every document can be notarized remotely. Some receiving parties, counties, and institutions still require a traditional in-person notarization. Check with whoever is receiving your document before booking a remote session, and if in doubt, we can meet in person instead.

Frequently asked

Remote Online Notarization questions

You join a scheduled video session on a secure platform. Your identity is verified electronically, you sign the document on screen, and the notarial act is recorded as Washington requires for remote online notarization. The fee is $25 per notarial act.

Washington caps the notarial act itself at $15 for a traditional or electronic in-person notarization, and $25 for a remote online notarization. If we travel to you, a mobile travel fee is added based on distance, and evening, weekend, or rush appointments add a scheduling fee. Every figure is published on the pricing page and confirmed before your appointment.

Bring your current, government-issued photo ID and the complete document, unsigned. Washington notaries must witness the signature, so please do not sign ahead of time. If the document names additional signers or witnesses, they need to attend as well.

We are based in Tacoma and travel throughout the South Puget Sound, including Lakewood, University Place, Puyallup, Fife, Federal Way, and Gig Harbor. Travel fees are calculated by distance, so please confirm your address when you book.
