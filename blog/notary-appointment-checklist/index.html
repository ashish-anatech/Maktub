What to bring to your notary appointment

May 18, 20264 min read

A little preparation keeps a notarization focused, efficient, and free of avoidable delays.

Bring valid identification

Every signer should bring an acceptable, current government-issued photo ID. The name on the ID should match the name on the document. If the name has changed, bring the supporting documentation your receiving organization requests.

Bring the complete document

Review the paperwork before the appointment. Do not sign it in advance unless the receiving party has specifically instructed you to do so. A notary may need to watch the signing.

  • All pages of the document, including attachments
  • Any instructions from the agency, title company, or attorney
  • Names and contact information for required witnesses

Important: A notary verifies identity and witnesses signatures; they cannot provide legal advice or decide what document you need.

Confirm witnesses and signer readiness

If witnesses are required, arrange them before the appointment and make sure they meet the document's requirements. Each signer should understand the document and be willing to sign without pressure.

Before you book

Tell us the document type, number of signers, location, and desired appointment time. We can then confirm the right service and provide a clear estimate.
