Who this is for

Estate documents tend to arrive at difficult moments: a diagnosis, a move into care, a family finally sitting down together. The paperwork is often prepared by an attorney and the signing is the last step, but it is the step that has to be done correctly.

This is the right service if you are signing a will, a trust, a power of attorney, or a healthcare directive, and you would rather the appointment be unhurried than efficient. Visits to homes, hospitals, and care facilities are routine.

What We Notarize

The paperwork behind a plan

Estate documents tend to arrive at difficult moments: a diagnosis, a move into care, a family finally sitting down together. The appointment is paced accordingly. Nobody is rushed toward a signature.

Appointments can take place at home, at a hospital or care facility, or at an attorney's office, whichever is easiest for the signer.

  • Wills and codicils
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Durable and healthcare powers of attorney
  • Advance healthcare directives
  • Deed transfers and beneficiary designations

How We Approach It

Care that goes beyond the seal

Unhurried appointments

Time is set aside so signers can read, ask, and sign at their own pace.

Bedside and facility visits

Hospital and nursing home appointments are available; travel is $60 to $90 plus the notary fee.

Awareness and willingness

Every signer must be acting freely and understand that they are signing. That is a requirement, not a formality.

Discretion by default

Family circumstances stay private. We are there to notarize, not to comment.

A notary is not a substitute for an attorney. Maktub Mobile Notary performs notarial acts only. We cannot draft your documents, advise which instrument you need, or explain what a provision means. Please have estate documents prepared or reviewed by a licensed Washington attorney.

Your money, before you commit

Fees you can check before you call
