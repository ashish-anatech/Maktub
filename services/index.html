Ways to work with us

Choose how we meet

You come to us

The simplest option, and the cheapest: you pay the notarial act fee and nothing else. $15 per signature for a traditional or electronic in-person notarization.

We come to you

Home, office, hospital or care facility. A mobile travel fee is added to the notarial fee: $35 within about 10 miles, $50 for 10 to 20 miles, $70 for 20 to 30 miles, and $60 to $90 for hospital and nursing home visits.

We meet online

A scheduled, recorded video session with electronic identity verification. $25 per notarial act and no travel fee, provided the party receiving your document accepts a remote online notarization.
